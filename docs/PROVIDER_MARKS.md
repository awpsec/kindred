# Composer provider marks

Provider marks identify the bot's configured provider, independently of its model. Codex retains the existing OpenAI assets; custom providers retain the neutral network symbol. SVG masks use Kindred's foreground color in both themes.

- OpenRouter: current OR mark from [OpenRouter's official sign-in kit](https://github.com/OpenRouterTeam/sign-in-with-openrouter/blob/main/public/openrouter-logo.svg), matching its [July 2026 brand refresh](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/brand-refresh/).
- Kimi: K-only symbol from [Moonshot AI's official brand guide](https://moonshotai.github.io/Branding-Guide/scenarios/04-k-only/k-only-dark.svg).
- Claude: Claude asterisk from [LobeHub Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/claude.svg), under the MIT license (copyright LobeHub). The asset identifies Claude, rather than Anthropic's company wordmark.

All marks remain trademarks of their respective owners. Assets are bundled locally; displaying a composer mark does not make an external request.
