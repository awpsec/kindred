# Kindred 0.90.3

A smoother conversation, with activity kept together and easier to follow.

- Adjacent jobs, reminders, artifact updates and teammate chats share a compact activity carousel.
- Long jobs retain their stacked appearance, with a subtle bot-colored outline while active and smoother expansion.
- Progress messages arrive smoothly before gathering into a longer job; the live activity indicator stays steady as work changes.
- Opening bot details keeps the name tag centered and moves the conversation and composer smoothly. Click the name tag again to close the pane.

Drafts and reading position are preserved while navigating activity and opening details.
